# Camera framing, occlusion and interior transition

## Required contract

Extend CameraFollow2_5D while preserving serialized target, offset, focusOffset and smoothTime names and types. Frame authored maps/interiors with tunable profiles and bounds. Snap/reseed on map transitions, teleports and replacement targets to avoid traversing off-map gaps. Preserve a fixed 2.5D angle. Optional occlusion must protect the player and important props, avoid permanent shared-material mutation and restore renderer state on transition/disable. Reduced-motion and overlay behavior must remain coherent. Provisional settings require visual comfort and collision acceptance in Play Mode.

## Implemented

- Existing target/offset/focusOffset/smoothTime serialized names/types/defaults remain compatible. Camera angle now derives solely from offset minus focusOffset, so smoothing and bounds do not rotate the view. Perspective distance scaling retains that angle; attached orthographic camera size scales too and restores on disable.
- Camera-owned per-map profiles resolve exact authored map IDs through the existing explicit loader or its instance. B01.Ground/B02.Ground/B03.Ground/B04.Ground receive provisional 0.75 distance scale, two-cell anchor inset and grid-bound clamping. Other maps retain baseline framing; clampUnprofiledMaps is opt-in. Grid-local bounds use existing BoundsMin/BoundsSize/CellSize and InverseTransformPoint/TransformPoint, honoring translated, rotated and scaled grids. Excessive inset collapses small rooms to center. Bounds clamp the follow anchor to authored cell centers; they do not guarantee the entire camera frustum stays inside geometry.
- SetTarget(Transform), SnapToTarget(), GridMover.Teleported subscriptions and a configurable four-world-unit displacement fallback reseed position/velocity rather than flying between distant targets/maps. Loader/area/map/grid reference changes also reseed on the next camera LateUpdate. Same-map rebuild/portal/save spawns use the existing mover teleport event. OnDisable unsubscribes; OnEnable explicitly rebinds even after disabled retargeting.
- SetReducedMotion(bool) immediately clears velocity/reseeds and bypasses smoothing while enabled. Camera consumes no input and changes no dialogue/overlay locks. Teleport and setting transitions snap even while gameplay time is paused.
- Optional default-off occlusion uses explicit CameraOccluder collider markers and authored renderer lists, a reusable 32-hit spherecast buffer and protectedRenderers. Target/mover hierarchy and explicitly protected props are excluded. Renderer.forceRenderingOff values are captured and restored when line of sight clears, target/map/grid changes, occlusion toggles off or camera disables. No material, shader, collider, Renderer.enabled or gameplay state mutation occurs.
- Deterministic math has eight NUnit case executions; seven Unity tests cover replacement/reduced-motion, transformed grid bounds, short mover teleports and disabled retarget/re-enable, fixed-angle/discontinuity follow, authored map-profile switch/fallback/restore, occlusion exit/replacement/toggle/disable/material preservation and protected/already-hidden renderer state. All new Assets files/directories have unique matching metadata.
