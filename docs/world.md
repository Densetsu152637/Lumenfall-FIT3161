# World and locations

The opening chapter takes place across a walled academy campus and the forest immediately beyond its west gate. The areas below define the intended player-accessible spaces and their narrative purpose.

For a detailed proposed expansion, see the [building and room plan](location-plan.md) and the [academy game map and creative rationale](maps/academy-map.md). These documents develop the layout without making every proposed interior a Chapter 1 production requirement.

## Forest

The forest is the large starting area and a recurring meeting place for Orio. It provides space for early exploration, environmental puzzles, and small NPC interactions.

## Courtyard

The campus commons is a large open area within the academy wall. It contains a central fountain, bushes, seating, and an assembly area associated with Cinna Cirra's orientation duties. Paths allow players to choose between separate buildings. The north, south, and east gates are reserved for future map expansion.

## Academy buildings

The campus contains separate arrival, teaching, shared-facilities, and Sirius dormitory buildings. Classrooms may vary in detail, but those differences are currently a low-priority production concern. The student dining room, library, spell-practice area, and a larger student common room are proposed shared facilities; their final scope depends on production time. Outdoor paths suggest navigation and do not require building-to-building corridors.

The dormitory contains a transport point that can connect students to classrooms. The mechanism is intended to function as a magical waypoint, with its interaction and travel rules to be specified during implementation. Walking across the commons remains available.

## Sirius dormitory

Sirius is the dormitory wing associated with Stella. Its shared common area is designed as a comfortable place for students to meet, converse, and rest.

The player reaches the group's room by speaking with a friend who leads them there. The room is shared by five students. In Chapter 1, the player's private room is the only private dorm space that requires access.

## Chapter 1 route

The intended sequence for meeting the principal friends is:

1. Stella
2. Cinna Cirra
3. Orio
4. Katrina

This sequence may be revised if quest dependencies or level flow require it.
