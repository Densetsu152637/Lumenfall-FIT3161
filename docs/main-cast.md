# Main cast and opening chapter

The four principal friends join the player during the opening chapter. Names in parentheses are current contributor references.

## Orio (Nick)

Orio is a clever, reliable student who becomes serious when circumstances require it. He is relaxed, frequently late, and enthusiastic about games, automation, and optimisation. Mentioning the game _Spellio_ can lead to an extended explanation.

He usually wears a tropical summer outfit. Flip-flops are required, and his expression should remain relaxed. Orio is particularly effective with one spell, but his confidence and casual manner can make him overlook practical concerns. His story quest is expected to introduce the forest and Rotara. A possible motivation is that he has misplaced a game console; this remains under consideration.

## Stella (Vi)

Stella is a fashionable, academically ambitious student who prefers rules and often assumes leadership. She is intelligent, whimsical, anxious, and frequently tired; she also likes plushies and wants to become a magical engineer.

She has moved from another city and is still adapting. Her controlling parents and difficulty accepting other people's opinions contribute to her headstrong personality. Her development arc centres on relaxing her standards and accepting help from friends.

Her outfit should be neat and fashionable. Combat boots, a red bow, and regular glasses are required. Her story quests introduce Ampere and Weave through her engineering assignment.

## Katrina (Jaz)

Katrina is ambitious, hard-working, and strongly interested in wealth, fashion, and celebrity culture. She is an enthusiastic fan of the idol Xiubin. She grew up without much privilege and does not always feel understood by her family.

Her outfit requires a long white skirt and white-and-blue sneakers. Glasses are optional; the top may be a T-shirt with a cardigan. Her character arc and the specific personal item in her story quest remain to be defined.

## Cinna Cirra (Len)

Cinna Cirra is a kind, nervous, and idealistic student who enjoys clouds, the sky, cute things, and androgynous clothing. They are book-smart but inexperienced with practical life outside their privileged upbringing, and they are still working out where they belong.

Their clothing should be loose and comfortable, such as long trousers with a T-shirt and jacket. Their development arc should challenge their sheltered assumptions while preserving their kindness. Their story quests introduce Vis and Glaciate.

## Opening chapter

The player begins with orientation and attempts to find the dormitory. The receptionist says the key has been passed to another student, so the player follows a chain of conversations and character quests to locate it. After meeting the dorm-mates, the group returns to the receptionist, who is confused because they have not seen any of the students before and gives them the key directly.

The group discusses the contradiction, but exhaustion ends the investigation for the night. The player returns to the dormitory and goes to sleep. Chapter 1 ends when the player wakes and the screen brightens, echoing the opening sequence.

Between main quests, the player can explore the academy grounds and solve puzzles. Small NPC interactions and side quests are planned, while replayable activities and reset behaviour remain open design decisions.
