# Academy game map and creative rationale

## Reading this map

This is the proposed spatial interpretation of [World and locations](../world.md). The [building and room inventory](../location-plan.md) defines every location identifier, access category, and floor assignment.

North is up. The diagrams show placement and movement, not scale. B01-B04 are separate buildings inside one wall. Outdoor paths are navigation suggestions; connection diagrams describe direct walking routes only within buildings.

## Single-storey interiors

All four buildings are ground-floor maps. B01 is one open reception and student-services hall; B02 is one teaching hall with puzzle zones; B03 is one large dining, study, and practice hall; and B04 is one shared common/group-living hall. The player's sleeping area is a ground-floor private room whose door can use the residential key. All travel stays on the ground level.

## Creative explanation

### The commons makes the campus readable

The fountain is the organising landmark. Players returning from the forest or leaving a classroom can locate themselves before choosing their next destination. Separate buildings establish a simple relationship: arrive near the east gate, learn in the northwest, use shared facilities in the north, and rest in the southeast.

The commons accommodates Cinna Cirra's public orientation responsibilities without inventing a separate assembly building. The trial strip sits near B02 so sliding and trap activity can be bounded against the perimeter, clear of the central walking paths.

### The forest becomes familiar before it becomes a quest destination

The opening walk introduces the forest trail before the player meets Orio. Returning through the east gate changes the purpose of a known place rather than asking the player to learn another region. Orio's glade is one short branch from the main trail: secluded enough for his improvised mechanism, but close enough to avoid a long compulsory detour.

The brook provides an optional pause. The blocked northern path gives the forest visual depth without requiring a larger playable wilderness in the opening chapter.

### Stella belongs beside practical teaching spaces

The engineering classroom is the first teaching room reached from reception. Its position makes Stella a natural first encounter, and the adjacent project room supports her second assignment if it is built. Her tasks remain within the teaching range, leaving the forest associated with Orio and the orientation grounds with Cinna Cirra.

### Katrina brings the route toward home

The garden walk lies beside Sirius. Meeting Katrina there moves the final introduction toward the dormitory while retaining a short return to reception. Benches and planting alcoves support a contained recovery search; the design does not depend on choosing her lost item yet.

Keeping the Lost Echoes search local lets the player's attention remain on Katrina's reluctance to explain the item's importance. A hidden section, if later required, can be a small garden recess rather than an additional building or extensive tunnel.

### Reception anchors the opening mystery

The same reception hall hosts both versions of the key conversation. Returning through a recognisable doorway makes the receptionist's contradiction more immediate: the location is stable even though the account of events is not. The hall stays close to the courtyard and dormitory so this return does not become a lengthy walk.

### Sirius changes the pace at the end of the chapter

Movement contracts from forest paths and open grounds to a common area, a shared suite, and finally the player's bedroom. This creates a gradual shift from public activity to private rest. The player's courtyard-facing window allows familiar academy light to support the bright awakening described in the opening chapter, without requiring a literal sunrise direction.

The suite interpretation accommodates both the five-person shared room and the player's private room in the source. Other bedroom doors establish the friends' presence while keeping only the player's bedroom accessible in Chapter 1.

## Suggested blockout order

1. Build F01-F04, A01, reception, and the commons with simple open campus paths. Confirm the east gate, perimeter wall, and fountain are easy to recognise.
2. Add W01, W02, and W06. Walk the Stella-to-Cinna-to-Orio route and ensure the forest return gate is readable from both sides.
3. Add C03 and C04 with contained puzzle footprints. Confirm mandatory circulation remains passable during every puzzle state.
4. Add D01, D02, D04, D07, and D08. Test the locked suite door, friend-guided arrival, reception return, and final sleep route.
5. Connect N01 to complete the ground-floor loop. Represent optional and reserved rooms with labelled doors before building their interiors.
6. Add approved shared facilities and waypoint travel only after the walking route and Chapter 1 interactions are satisfactory.

This order produces a coherent opening map before committing to the full architectural inventory. The library, dining hall, practice hall, academy common room, extra classrooms, and private NPC rooms can be developed independently as production capacity allows.
